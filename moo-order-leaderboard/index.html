How to read this — and what it can't tell you

  • Signal is the honest column. It runs a two-sided Poisson test on each person against the group's own complaint rate for the selected range. noise means their gap from the average is within what chance produces at that volume. Over the full comparable window not one person clears significance — so treat rank order as unproven and use this for coaching and streaks, not scoring.
  • The poster is the preparer — confirmed with the team. The complaints sheet's Prepared by column names someone else on 16 of 102 photo-matched complaints (16%), flagged inline in the detail table. The photo is the better evidence, and that matters because the sheet is what deductions are charged against.
  • Yilobemo posted under three handles across device changes (Outpost Indiranagar, ~ Okharo, his own). They're merged here; any analysis that doesn't merge them undercounts him by roughly 700 orders.
  • Undocumented orders are complaints with no photo at all — the kitchen never posted them. They can't be attributed to a person, so they sit outside the leaderboard and are counted separately.
  • Match quality: the KOT join lands 102 of 125 complaints (81.6%) against a 5.8% random-collision floor. 23 have no photo to match — the order was never posted. A few orders share a 4-digit code inside the ±1 day window; those are marked ambiguous in the detail table.
  • Two people joined in August — James (HSR, from 11 Aug) and Adam (HSR, from 22 Aug). Short windows, so their rates are the least reliable on the board. Michael and Aben stopped posting on 4 and 5 Aug.
