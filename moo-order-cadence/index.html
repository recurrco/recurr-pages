When to call someone lapsed

No cliff exists

Given a customer has been silent for X days, the chance they order again in the next 90.

P(returns within 90 days | silent for X days)
Decays smoothly — roughly halving every 45 days of silence.
There is no natural breakpoint, so "lapsed" is a business choice rather than a data fact. 30 days is the defensible line — it is where the return chance drops below one in four, and 78% of all repeat orders have already happened by then.

09

Is cadence improving? No — it is stable

And untested

By acquisition cohort, only cohorts with ≥90 days observed.

Share of each cohort placing a second order within 7 days
Dashed line = the 12.1% average across the 12 cohorts since Jun 2025.
The April 2025 launch cohort was exceptional — 18.2% reordered within a week, against 10–15% ever since. Early adopters. Since mid-2025 the 7-day reorder rate has held a flat band and the median 1→2 gap has sat at 9–13 days. Flat, not decaying. The honest read: cadence is a stable property of the product, and nothing MOO has done in 14 months has moved it — which also means nothing has ever been tried on it.

What this changes

Five things follow

1
The retention window is 7 days, not 30
Week one carries 44% of a new customer's 90-day repeat probability, and a second order inside a week is worth 2.4× one after 90 days. Every retention rupee should be spent in the first seven days.
2
A subscription is the obvious product
64% of 20+ order customers already order on three or more consecutive days; 435 customers run a ≤2-day median cadence. They are hand-rebuying a daily habit through an aggregator, at full commission.
3
Nudge by hour, never by weekday
69% daypart concentration against a 49.5% baseline is a real anchor. 36.5% weekday concentration against 33.9% is noise. Personalise on the customer's own clock.
4
Investigate why the near-daily tribe dies at 88 days
The highest-frequency segment has the shortest lifespan of any tribe — 2.7 active months. Burnout, price, or variety fatigue; we do not know which, and it is the single most valuable unknown here.
5
Nothing can act on any of this yet
rista_dim.dim_customer holds zero rows and fact_orders.customer_key is NULL on all 108,322 orders. The identity exists only inside raw JSON. Populating it is the single build task between here and a standing cadence dashboard.
Method. Every figure computed in SQL against rista_raw.sales_transactions on 25 Aug 2026 (warehouse bptkkontvzqtdbdntwnx), filtered to channel='Zomato' and status='Closed'.
