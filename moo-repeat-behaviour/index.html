01 · Repeat rate

Repeat rate, on both denominators

62.7% of orders are a repeat purchase Order-weighted. 11,977 of 19,112 orders were placed by a customer who had ordered before.
30.6% of customers ordered more than once Customer-weighted. 2,786 of 9,118 customers placed a second order.

Both measure the same 19,112 orders. A minority of customers generates the majority of orders — the sections below describe that minority.

Growth in acquisition normally dilutes repeat share, as new customers enter the denominator. Over these 14 months it rose. Nov 25 (greyed) is the highest month in the series but falls on the lowest order volume of the period, and is excluded from the comparison.

Zomato only. All 55,452 Swiggy orders carry no customer identifier at all — nor do Magicpin, Ownly or DotPe. Zomato is roughly half of aggregator order volume; the behaviour of the other half is not yet measurable.
Identity is Zomato's hash, not a verified person. One account shared by a household reads as one customer; one person on two accounts reads as two.
The ladder's tail is understated. Steps past order 8 are right-censored — customers still inside their window haven't had time to place the next order. The true numbers are higher, not lower.
AOV is bill amount (₹301), not the deck's gross AOV (₹362.70). The two are different bases and are not comparable.
