What a box is

Three movements, and Goodera does the heavy lifting on all three. You pick the box and the date. Everything else — sourcing, shipping, the nonprofit on the other end, the numbers afterwards — is already handled.

  1. It arrives

    Flat-packed, counted and labelled, at every office you name. One box per pair of hands, or a thousand across eight countries in the same week.

  2. Your team builds it

    Thirty to a hundred and twenty minutes, in the office, outdoors, at a kiosk in the lobby or fully virtual. No prior skill assumed, instructions in the lid.

  3. Someone receives it

    A vetted nonprofit collects, distributes and confirms. You get an impact report with the count, the region and the partner named.

Anatomy of a box

Anyone can ship a kit. What arrives in a Goodera box is four things bonded together — and the last one is the reason a CSR team can put it in a report.

Layer one

The kit

Every component counted, sourced and flat-packed. Nothing to buy, nothing to prepare, nothing left over.

Layer two

The volunteering activity

A facilitated session with a run sheet, a host and a duration. Booked for one office or forty-four countries on the same day.

Layer three

The nonprofit partner

Vetted, named and matched before the box ships — drawn from 50,000+ partners across 100+ countries. The box has a destination before it has a build date.

Layer four

The impact report

What was built, how many, which region, which partner received it. Quantified, not described. This is the layer a kit bought off a shelf can never carry.

Same boxes. Different moments.

The catalogue does not change by occasion. What changes is the volume, the date and who is in the room.

Every box has somewhere to go

A box without a recipient is a craft activity. Goodera attaches the nonprofit before the box ships — matched on cause, region and what the partner can actually receive and distribute this month.

Match

Cause and region

You pick a cause area. Goodera proposes vetted partners in the regions where your offices sit.

Confirm

Capacity, not just intent

Partners confirm they can receive and distribute the volume before a single box is packed.

Report

Named in the numbers

The partner is named in your impact report, alongside the count and the region served.
